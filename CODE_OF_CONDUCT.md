# Code of Conduct / 行動規範

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not people.

This project is maintained by one person in their spare time, in three languages, and most people
who show up will be strangers to each other. That works only if everyone extends a little
patience — especially across a language barrier, where terseness reads as rudeness and a
translation slip reads as an insult.

## What is expected

- Assume good faith. A blunt comment from someone writing in their second or third language is
  almost never the attack it can look like.
- Keep criticism about the work: the code, the design, the decision. Not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that "no" is a complete answer. This is a small project with a deliberately narrow scope;
  a feature can be a good idea and still not belong here.
- English, 日本語 and italiano are all welcome in issues and pull requests.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments; personal or political attacks; publishing anyone's
private information; sexualised language or imagery; and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to **support@copaky.app** with `[copaky-conduct]` in the subject.
Reports are read by the maintainer alone and kept confidential. You will get a reply within 7 days.

Because there is only one maintainer, there is no committee and no appeal to a wider body — that is
an honest limitation of a project this size, not a policy. If your report concerns the maintainer,
you are entitled to say so publicly, and GitHub's own [reporting
channels](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) exist independently of this project.

Responses available: a private word, a public correction, removal of comments, or a block from the
repository, depending on severity.

## Scope

This applies in the project's own spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit
conversation — and when someone represents the project in public.

## 行動規範（日本語）

**要点：** 礼儀を守り、相手は善意で動いていると考え、人ではなくコードを批評してください。

このプロジェクトは一人が余暇に、三つの言語で運営しています。言語の壁越しでは、簡潔な物言いが
無礼に、翻訳のずれが侮辱に見えてしまいます。少しの寛容さが前提です。

**期待すること**：善意を前提とすること。批評は成果物（コード・設計・判断）に向けること。「いいえ」も
完全な回答だと受け入れること — 良い提案でも、意図的に狭く保っているこのプロジェクトには
合わないことがあります。issue や PR は英語・日本語・イタリア語のいずれでも歓迎します。

**認められないこと**：ハラスメント、侮辱、見下す発言、個人攻撃、私的情報の公開、性的な言動、
議論の継続的な妨害。

**報告**：件名に `[copaky-conduct]` を入れて **support@copaky.app** へ非公開でご連絡ください。
メンテナ本人のみが読み、秘密として扱います。7日以内に返信します。メンテナが一人であるため
委員会や上位機関への申し立ての仕組みはありません（規模ゆえの正直な限界です）。報告内容が
メンテナ自身に関わる場合は、公に指摘していただいて構いませんし、GitHub 自体の
[報告窓口](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)は本プロジェクトとは独立して利用できます。
